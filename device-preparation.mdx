Before configuring a phone proxy, disable location services and adjust the device's language, region, date, and time to the target country of your proxy IP. Follow these requirements for both relay and native proxies.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Disable location services">
        Open **Settings**, find **Location Services** under **Privacy**, and turn location services off.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/tutorials/m_ios_sr_1.png" alt="Turn off location services in iOS settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Adjust the date, time, language, and region">
        Under **General**, set the phone's date, time, and time zone to match your target country. Choose a language other than Simplified Chinese, preferably the target country's language. Set the region to your target country.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/tutorials/m_ios_sr_2.png" alt="Adjust the iOS date, time, language, and region" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Adjust the date, time, language, and region">
        In your phone settings, set the date, time, and time zone to match your target country. Choose a language other than Simplified Chinese, preferably the target country's language. Set the region to your target country.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_1.png" alt="Adjust the Android date, time, language, and region" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Disable location services">
        Find **Location** in your phone settings and turn location services off.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_2.png" alt="Turn off location services in Android settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Continue setup

After preparing your device, return to the [tutorial guide](/introduction) and choose your proxy mode and client. Once connected, visit [whoer.net](https://whoer.net) in your browser and check that the exit IP matches your expectations.

<Note>
  For a native proxy, you also need a working overseas network connection and a MiyaIP SOCKS5 node. See [proxy modes](/proxy-types) for the prerequisites.
</Note>
