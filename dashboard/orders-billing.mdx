The **Orders & Billing** (订单与账单) section of the MiyaIP dashboard is your hub for everything financial — checking your account balance, topping up credits, reviewing past orders, and managing auto-renewal discounts. You can also find the referral program here, which lets you earn rewards by sharing MiyaIP with others. This page explains each feature available in this section and how to use them.

## Finding Orders & Billing

Click **订单与账单 (Orders & Billing)** in the left sidebar of the MiyaIP dashboard. This takes you to a unified view of your purchase history and account financial status.

<Note>
  If you run into any billing issues or have questions about your orders, click the **反馈问题 (Feedback)** button at the top of the dashboard to contact the MiyaIP support team directly.
</Note>

## Account Balance and Recharging

Your current account balance is displayed in the **top-right corner** of the dashboard. To add funds to your account:

1. Click the **充值 (Recharge)** button located in the top-right area.
2. Select your preferred top-up amount or enter a custom value.
3. Complete the payment using your chosen payment method.

Your balance is used for all proxy purchases, renewals, and auto-renewal charges across your account.

## Product Status Indicator

At the top of the Orders & Billing section, a **product status indicator** gives you a quick overview of the current state of your active subscriptions. Use this to spot any services that may need attention, such as proxies that are nearing expiry or renewals that are pending.

## Auto-Renewal Discounts

If you are a **VIP member**, enabling auto-renewal on your proxy IPs gives you a **55% discount** on each renewal charge. This discount is applied automatically whenever an auto-renewal is processed. You can toggle auto-renewal for individual IPs from the [Manage Proxies](/dashboard/manage-proxies) page.

## Referral and Invite Program

MiyaIP offers a referral rewards program — **邀请好友赚奖励 (Invite Friends, Earn Rewards)**. When you share your unique MiyaIP referral link and a new user signs up and makes a purchase, you earn rewards that are credited to your account.

To access your referral link and track your referral earnings, look for the invite program banner or link within the Orders & Billing section of your dashboard.

## Language Switcher

In the **top-right corner** of the dashboard — alongside the recharge button, notifications, and your profile menu — you will find a **language switcher** that toggles the dashboard interface between **中文 (Chinese)** and **EN (English)**. Switch to your preferred language at any time without affecting your account settings.
