---

sidebarTitle: "Android NekoBox"

---

Download and install [NekoBox 1.4.2](https://github.com/MatsuriDayo/NekoBoxForAndroid/releases/tag/1.4.2), then follow these steps to configure your MiyaIP relay proxy.

## Prepare your Android phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the date, time, language, and region">
    Find **General** and set your phone's date and time to those of your target country or region. Choose a language other than Simplified Chinese, preferably your target country's language, and set the region to your target country or region.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_1.png" alt="Adjust the date, time, language, and region in Android settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off location">
    Find **Location** and turn it off to prevent apps from reading your location.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_2.png" alt="Turn off location in Android settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your relay proxy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add menu">
    Open the NekoBox home screen and tap the **plus button** in the top-right corner.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_3.png" alt="Tap the plus button in the top-right corner of NekoBox" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the QR scanner">
    Select **Scan QR code** from the add menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_4.png" alt="Select Scan QR code from the NekoBox add menu" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and import the NekoBox QR code">
    On the MiyaIP website, open the relay proxy QR code for your purchased IP, select **NekoBox**, and scan its QR code to save the node.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/2.png" alt="View the relay proxy NekoBox QR code on the MiyaIP website" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and test latency">
    Select the static node you just saved and tap the **paper plane button** at the bottom to connect. Tap the **pink area** to test handshake latency. If the test fails or times out, turn off the **paper plane button** and reconnect.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_nb_6.png" alt="Select a NekoBox node, connect, and test latency in the pink area" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>

    Once connected, open [whoer.net](https://whoer.net) in your browser. Check that the exit IP and region match your configured MiyaIP proxy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also watch the [NekoBox video reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kToFOqzVfh4&t=836s).
