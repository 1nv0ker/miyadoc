---
title: "Set up a relay proxy on Android with v2rayNG"
sidebarTitle: "Android v2rayNG"
description: "Scan your MiyaIP relay proxy QR code, connect with v2rayNG on Android, test latency, and check your exit IP."
---

Download and install the app from the [official v2rayNG releases page](https://github.com/2dust/v2rayNG/releases). You need an overseas network connection to access the download page.

Before connecting, follow [Device preparation](/device-preparation) to adjust your phone settings.

## Connect your relay proxy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the QR scanner">
    Open the v2rayNG home screen, tap the **plus button** in the top-right corner, and select **Scan QR code**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_v2_1.png" alt="Select Scan QR code from the v2rayNG add menu" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and import the v2rayNG QR code">
    On the MiyaIP website, open the relay proxy QR code for your purchased IP, select **V2ray**, and scan its QR code to import the node.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/3.png" alt="View the relay proxy V2ray QR code on the MiyaIP website" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and check your exit IP">
    Select the imported node, tap the **start button** at the bottom, and tap the **blank area** to test connection latency.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_and_v2_3.png" alt="Start the v2rayNG proxy and tap the blank area to test the connection" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>

    After the test, open the Google browser and visit [whoer.net](https://whoer.net). Check that the exit IP and region match your configured MiyaIP proxy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
