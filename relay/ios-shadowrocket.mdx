---
title: "Set up a relay proxy on iPhone with Shadowrocket"
sidebarTitle: "iOS Shadowrocket"
description: "Prepare your iPhone, scan your MiyaIP relay proxy QR code, and use Shadowrocket to connect and check your exit IP."
---

Use a US Apple ID to download **Shadowrocket**, then follow these steps to configure your MiyaIP relay proxy.

## Prepare your iPhone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn off location services">
    Open **Settings**, find **Location Services** under **Privacy**, and turn it off to prevent apps from reading your location.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_ios_sr_1.png" alt="Turn off location services in iPhone settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date, time, language, and region">
    Find **General** and set your phone's date and time to those of your target country or region. Choose a language other than Simplified Chinese, preferably your target country's language, and set the region to your target country or region.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_ios_sr_2.png" alt="Adjust the date, time, language, and region in iPhone General settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your relay proxy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan and import the Shadowrocket QR code">
    Open Shadowrocket and tap the **scan button** in the top-left corner. On the MiyaIP website, open the relay proxy QR code for your purchased IP, select **Shadowrocket**, and scan its QR code to import the node.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_ios_sr_3.png" alt="Tap the scan button in the top-left corner of Shadowrocket" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    After importing the node, tap the **rocket button** at the top to start the proxy connection.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/m_ios_sr_4.png" alt="Tap the rocket button at the top of Shadowrocket to connect" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your exit IP">
    Enter [whoer.net](https://whoer.net) in the Google browser's address bar. Check that the displayed exit IP and region match your configured MiyaIP proxy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
