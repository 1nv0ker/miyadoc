MiyaIP is a residential proxy service that provides real, verified residential IP addresses sourced from locations all over the world. Whether you need a stable long-term identity for account management, a rotating IP pool for data collection, or a dedicated IP optimized for social media live streaming, MiyaIP gives you flexible options that fit your workflow — all accessible from your mobile device or desktop in minutes.

## How MiyaIP Works

MiyaIP supports two distinct usage modes depending on whether you already have access to an overseas network.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Relay Proxy (中转代理)" icon="tower-broadcast">
    MiyaIP provides the entire transit relay chain. You do **not** need your own overseas VPN or proxy environment. Simply configure the provided server address and credentials in your client app, and MiyaIP handles the routing from your device to the residential IP exit node.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Native Proxy (原始代理)" icon="network-wired">
    You supply your own first-hop overseas network connection (e.g., an existing VPN or proxy). MiyaIP then provides a SOCKS5 residential IP as the **second hop**, layering a genuine residential identity on top of your existing connection for maximum authenticity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## AdsPower Integration

MiyaIP integrates seamlessly with **AdsPower**, the leading anti-detect browser for multi-account management. By assigning a unique MiyaIP residential IP to each browser profile, you ensure that every account operates from a distinct, authentic residential identity — dramatically reducing the risk of detection or bans.

## Dashboard Features

Once you log in to the MiyaIP dashboard, you can manage all of your proxy assets in one place:

- **View purchased IPs** — see all active IPs, their regions, and expiry dates at a glance
- **QR code export** — generate a QR code for any IP configuration and scan it directly into your client app
- **Manual renewal** — extend the validity of any IP on demand before it expires
- **Auto-renewal** — enable automatic renewal so your critical IPs never go offline unexpectedly
