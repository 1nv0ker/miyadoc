## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the MiyaIP Dashboard">
    Sign in at [miyaip.com](https://miyaip.com) and navigate to **Proxy → Static Residential** (or your purchased proxy type) to find your IPs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your device">
    Before connecting, disable location services and set your device language and time zone to match your target region. See [Device Preparation](/device-preparation) for full instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your setup mode">
    - **Relay Proxy** — scan the QR code directly from the MiyaIP dashboard (Shadowrocket, Nekobox, or V2rayNG). No extra overseas network needed.
    - **Native Proxy** — manually enter your SOCKS5 credentials from the dashboard and chain them through your existing overseas VPN.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your IP">
    Open a browser and navigate to [whoer.net](https://whoer.net) to confirm the proxy is active and your IP matches the target region.
  </Step>
</Steps>
