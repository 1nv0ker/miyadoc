Native Proxy mode (原始代理) lets you chain MiyaIP's SOCKS5 residential IP as a **second hop** on top of your existing overseas VPN or proxy network. Instead of routing traffic directly from your device to the residential IP, your first-hop overseas connection acts as the entry tunnel, and MiyaIP's static residential IP becomes the clean exit node that websites see. This guide walks you through configuring that two-layer chain inside Shadowrocket on iPhone.

<Warning>
  This mode requires an active first-layer overseas network (VPN or proxy) already installed and running on your device **before** you begin. If you do not have an overseas first-hop network, Native Proxy mode will not work correctly.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have the following ready:

- **Shadowrocket** installed on your iPhone (available on the App Store).
- An **existing overseas VPN or proxy** already configured and active on your device — this is your first hop.
- Your **MiyaIP SOCKS5 credentials**: IP address, port, username, and password. Find these in the MiyaIP dashboard under **Static Residential → your IP row**.
- Location Services **disabled** on your device.
- Your iPhone's **language, region, and date/time** changed to match the target country.

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import the MiyaIP SOCKS5 node">
    Open Shadowrocket. Tap the **QR code scan icon** in the top-left corner and scan the SOCKS5 node QR code provided in your MiyaIP dashboard. This imports the static residential IP node into Shadowrocket automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the node settings">
    Locate the newly imported MiyaIP node in your server list. Tap the **exclamation mark (!)** on the right side of the node row to open its detailed settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Proxy Via">
    Inside the node settings, scroll down and tap **"Proxy Via"** (代理通过). This option tells Shadowrocket to route the connection to this node through another proxy — your existing overseas network.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your overseas network as the first hop">
    From the list of available proxies, select your **own overseas VPN or proxy** as the proxy-through. This designates it as the first hop that MiyaIP's SOCKS5 node will tunnel through.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify settings and save">
    Return to the node settings screen and confirm that the protocol is set to **SOCKS5** and that all proxy details (IP, port, username, password) are correct. Tap **Save** to apply your changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect with the static IP node">
    Back on the Shadowrocket home screen, select the MiyaIP static IP as your **active node**. Set **Global Route** to **Proxy**, then tap the **rocket button** at the top of the screen to connect.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your connection">
    Open your browser and navigate to [whoer.net](https://whoer.net) to confirm that your visible IP address matches the MiyaIP residential IP and that the location reflects the target country.
  </Step>
</Steps>
