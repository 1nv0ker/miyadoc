Use this guide if you already have an overseas network subscription and want to connect to a MiyaIP static residential **Native proxy** on Android.

Download and install NekoBox from the [NekoBox 1.4.2 release page](https://github.com/MatsuriDayo/NekoBoxForAndroid/releases/tag/1.4.2).

## Prepare your device

1. Open **Settings** and set your **time zone**, **language**, and **region** to match the proxy IP's target country or region. Menu names vary by Android device. Choose the target country's language and avoid using Simplified Chinese.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_1.png" alt="Adjust the date, time, language, and region on Android" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
</Frame>

2. Find **Location** in **Settings** and turn it off.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_2.png" alt="Turn off Location in Android settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
</Frame>

See [Device preparation](/device-preparation) for more details.

## Before you connect

- Install **NekoBox** on your Android phone and prepare a working overseas network subscription URL for your first network hop.
- Get the SOCKS5 credentials for your static residential **Native proxy** from the MiyaIP dashboard: **host, port, username, and password**.

## Configuration steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a node">
    Open NekoBox and tap the **plus button** in the upper-right corner of the home screen.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_3.png" alt="Tap the plus button in the upper-right corner of NekoBox" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Manual input">
    Tap **Manual input** in the add menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_4.png" alt="Select Manual input in the NekoBox add menu" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the SOCKS protocol">
    Tap **SOCKS** in the protocol list.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_5.png" alt="Select SOCKS in the NekoBox protocol list" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your MiyaIP proxy details">
    Enter a recognizable **Name**. Fill in your MiyaIP native proxy **host, port, username, and password**, confirm that you use **SOCKS5**, then tap **✓** in the upper-right corner to save.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_6.png" alt="Enter the MiyaIP SOCKS5 proxy details and save the node" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the side menu">
    Tap the **three-line menu button** in the upper-left corner.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_7.png" alt="Tap the menu button in the upper-left corner of NekoBox" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Groups">
    Tap **Groups** in the side menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_8.png" alt="Open Groups from the NekoBox side menu" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a group">
    Tap the **plus button** in the upper-right corner of the Groups page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_9.png" alt="Tap the plus button on the Groups page" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your overseas subscription">
    Enter a **group name** and change the **group type** to **Subscription**. Paste your existing overseas network subscription URL into **Subscription link**, then tap **✓** in the upper-right corner to save.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_10.png" alt="Create a subscription group with your existing overseas subscription URL" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the subscription group">
    Find the overseas subscription group you just created and tap **Edit**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_11.png" alt="Edit the overseas subscription group you just created" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Landing proxy">
    Tap **Landing proxy** in the group settings.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_12.png" alt="Open Landing proxy in the subscription group settings" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your MiyaIP static IP node">
    Select the **MiyaIP SOCKS5 node** you saved earlier as the group's Landing proxy.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_13.png" alt="Select the MiyaIP static IP node as the Landing proxy" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the side menu again">
    Tap the **three-line menu button** in the upper-left corner.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_14.png" alt="Open the NekoBox side menu again" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Config">
    Tap **Config** in the side menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_15.png" alt="Open the Config page from the NekoBox side menu" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and test latency">
    Select an **overseas network node** in the overseas subscription group. Tap the **paper airplane button** to connect, then tap the **pink area** to run a handshake latency test.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tutorials/o_and_nb_16.png" alt="Select an overseas node, connect, and tap the pink area to test latency" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "480px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your exit IP">
    Open [whoer.net](https://whoer.net) in your browser. Confirm that the displayed **IP address** matches the MiyaIP static residential IP you purchased and that the **country or region** is correct.

  </Step>
</Steps>
